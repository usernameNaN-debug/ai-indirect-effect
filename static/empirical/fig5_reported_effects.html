Effects refer to each respondent's chatbot use overall. Figure 2 defines the mutually exclusive groups using their finance responses.

Source: authors' survey of US chatbot users, fielded August 22–25, 2026 (n = 1,850 after quality screens).

Compared with respondents in the no-finance-use group, people who hire professionals less for money matters are 38 points more likely to report money saved, 8 points more likely to report incorrect information, and 10 points more likely to feel uneasy about data handling. Compared with finance users who cut only unpaid inputs, their savings rate is 16 points higher, while incorrect-information reports are equal.

Respondents cutting professional help are heavier chatbot users, and they report more of both gains and errors. Accuracy belongs alongside savings in any account of AI tax help. Tax-return errors create compliance problems, so the next survey will ask whether users verify AI-assisted returns.
