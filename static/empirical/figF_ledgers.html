The time ledger can be larger than the market ledger, even when the market barely moves

The IRS estimates that Americans spend about 2.1 billion hours a year on federal income tax returns and $49 billion out of pocket on software and preparers. This simulation compares two effects of AI tax help: household time saved and preparer revenue moved.

Annual value of two effects of AI tax help, in billions of dollars

Both bars are simulations. Adjust the inputs to compare household time savings with preparer revenue moved.

Time saved = filers × adoption × average filing hours × share of hours saved × value of an hour. Preparer revenue moved = professionally prepared returns × share switching to self-preparation × average fee. Average hours (12.6) and the IRS value of time ($21 per hour) are implied by the burden model's 2.129 billion hours, $48.7 billion out-of-pocket and $93.7 billion monetized burden for 168.8 million filers. The average fee is H&R Block's US assisted revenue per assisted return in fiscal 2026.

Sources: IRS Taxpayer Burden Model via Federal Register, December 19, 2024; IRS filing season statistics; H&R Block Form 10-K, fiscal 2026; BEA household production wage as used in Google ATLAS (2026).

At the midpoint of the adoption range, a 20% time saving returns about 68 million hours to filers each year, worth $1.4 billion at the IRS's value of time. A 1% simulated shift from professional preparation to self-filing moves about $170 million in preparer revenue. Household hours create a net resource gain. The fee mostly transfers from preparers to filers; the social gain comes from preparation time freed on both sides. GDP records paid transactions and has no line for unpaid household hours.

The IRS's 2.1 billion hours equal the full-time work of roughly one million people. Brynjolfsson and coauthors estimate that US consumers value access to generative AI at about $172 billion a year, against roughly $14 billion of global consumer spending. Tax filing gives this value-to-revenue gap a concrete measure of household time.
